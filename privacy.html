HIRELOOT

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 19 日。适用于 hireloot.com 网页版，以及 iPhone、Mac 上的 Hireloot App。

我们收集什么

  • 邮箱地址和密码：只在你注册账号时收集，用来登录。密码由 Supabase 加密保存，我们看不到明文。
  • 游戏存档：你写下的任务、面试记录、角色名字、武器收集等，用来在你的各个设备之间同步。

不登录（游客模式）时，存档只保存在你自己的设备上，不会上传。

我们不做什么

  • 不做广告，不卖数据，不和第三方分享你的数据。
  • 不做跨 App 或跨网站的追踪，没有第三方统计或分析工具。

数据存在哪里

账号和云存档保存在 Supabase 提供的数据库里，数据库设置了权限，每个人只能读写自己的存档。网页由 GitHub Pages 托管，经过 Cloudflare 网络传输，全程使用 HTTPS 加密。

删除你的数据

在网页版或 iPhone、Mac 的 Hireloot App 里，打开「设置」，点「删除账号」，你的账号和云存档会被立即永久删除。你也可以在 GitHub 上联系我们，帮你删除。游客模式的数据删除 App 或清除浏览器数据即可。

联系我们

对隐私有任何疑问，请在 GitHub Issues 留言。

Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026. Covers the web version at hireloot.com and the Hireloot app for iPhone and Mac.

What we collect

  • Email address and password, only when you create an account, so you can sign in. Passwords are stored hashed by Supabase; we never see them.
  • Your game save: the tasks, interviews, hero name and weapon collection you enter, so they sync across your devices.

In guest mode nothing is uploaded; your save stays on your device.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no selling or sharing your data with third parties.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no third-party analytics.

Where data is stored

Accounts and cloud saves are stored in a database run by Supabase, with access rules so each person can only read and write their own save. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. All traffic uses HTTPS.

Deleting your data

On the web or in the Hireloot app on iPhone or Mac, open Settings and choose “Delete account”. Your account and cloud save are deleted immediately and permanently. You can also ask us on GitHub to delete it for you. Guest data is removed by deleting the app or clearing your browser data.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub.
